What Are Corrugated Plastic Signs?

Let’s start with the basics. Corrugated plastic, often referred to as Coroplast (a brand name), is a lightweight yet sturdy material made of polypropylene—a type of plastic that’s both water- and weather-resistant.

It’s constructed similarly to corrugated cardboard, but instead of paper, it uses plastic fluting (those tiny vertical air pockets between two flat layers). That makes it:

  • Lightweight (easy to carry and install)
  • Durable (holds up in rain, wind, and sunshine)
  • Cost-effective (especially for bulk orders)

The most common thickness for signage? 4mm corrugated plastic signs—offering a balance of strength and affordability.

Ideal uses include:

  • Yard signs for businesses and services
  • Real estate and open house signs
  • Directional signage at events
  • Political or campaign signage
  • Event or school announcements

From small lawn signs to large corrugated plastic signs for outdoor visibility, they check every box.

Why Choose Corrugated Plastic Signs Over Other Materials?

Corrugated plastic isn’t just a convenient option—it’s a strategic one. Here’s how it stacks up against other signage materials:

  • Durability: Unlike cardboard or foam board, corrugated plastic won’t warp or wilt in bad weather. It thrives outdoors, making it perfect for Michigan’s hot summers, rainy springs, and everything in between.
  • Recyclability: Yes, you can recycle corrugated plastic signs. Look for facilities that accept polypropylene (#5 plastic), and dispose of old signs sustainably when your campaign or event ends.
  • Cost-Effective: Custom-size corrugated plastic signs are significantly cheaper than aluminum or acrylic while still offering great print quality and reusability.
  • Flexible Shapes and Sizes: From basic rectangles to die-cut corrugated plastic signs shaped like arrows, houses, or logos—you can customize exactly what fits your need and brand.
